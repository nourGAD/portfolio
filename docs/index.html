I'm a PMP®-certified Senior RAN Engineer and Technical Project Manager at Ericsson MMEA, where I mentor and certify the partner (ASP) teams that install, integrate, and commission radio sites across the Middle East & Africa.

My work sits between the field and the program: leading multi-team rollouts, resolving integration and commissioning issues across Ericsson, Huawei, and Nokia environments, and turning that knowledge into training that field engineers can act on — delivered bilingually in Arabic and English, on-site and remote. I've worked across every generation from 2G to 5G, including research and testing work on 6G.

Alongside delivery, I build the tools my teams actually use — MOP generators, productivity dashboards, and QA workflows — and I'm certified in Google Cloud (ACE) and Kubernetes (KCNA), bringing DevOps practice into telecom operations.
